    Privacy Notice

    Effective . Plain-language summary of what Street Fix collects and why.

    What we collect

    • Report details: the location you pin, photos, problem type, and notes. These are public record.
    • Contact details (optional): name and mobile number, so we can text you updates. Only city staff see them.
    • Account: your mobile number, if you sign in.

    Location

    We ask for your location only when you tap "Use my location" or start a report. We never track you in the background. You can always drag the map or type an address instead.

    Photos

    Photos are resized on your phone and their embedded location and camera data is removed before upload. Staff blur faces and license plates before a photo is shown publicly.

    What we don't do

    No ads, no advertising trackers, and we never sell your data. Contact details are not shared outside city departments, except as required by law.

    Retention

    Reports are kept as required by the city's records retention schedule. Contact details are deleted 2 years after a report is closed.

    Your choices

    You can report anonymously, download your data, or delete it from this device in Profile. To delete account data held by the city, call 311.

    Terms of Use

    Effective .

    Not for emergencies

    Street Fix is not monitored around the clock. If anyone is in danger, call 911. For urgent road hazards, call 311.

    Be accurate and respectful

    Only report real problems on public property. Don't include other people's personal information in notes or photos. Abusive, false, or duplicate-spam reports may be removed.

    Safety

    Never stand in traffic to take a photo, and don't use the app while driving.

    No guarantee

    Repair estimates are targets, not promises. Reporting a problem or filing a claim does not create liability for the city or guarantee payment.

    Public record

    Reports, photos, and notes may be released under public records law. Contact details are withheld where the law allows.
